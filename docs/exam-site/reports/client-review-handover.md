## What to look at

**Student A** (has bought the Full Mock package)
- Two finished exams with published results: an MCQ paper (35 of 50, correct answers shown) and a written paper (32 of 50, with the marker's comment and a marked PDF to download).
- One written paper handed in and still waiting for marks.
- A new 11 PM exam every night for the next seven days, alternating MCQ and written.

**Student B** (has bought the Written package)
- Two written papers handed in, waiting for marks.

**Student C** (has bought one written exam)
- Try buying the MCQ package. The payment page is the SSLCommerz test page, so use a test card and never a real one.

**Instructor**
- Sees two packages and four written papers waiting to be marked. Open one, write marks and a comment, upload a marked PDF.
- Cannot see the other packages.

**Staff**
- Students, payments (three paid, one failed), packages and exams.

**Master Admin** (your own account)
- After the instructor marks a paper, release the result. Then sign in as that student and see it appear.
- Publishing, corrections and archiving an exam are Master Admin actions.
- "Forgot password" does **not** work for the Master Admin account, by design. Keep that password safe; a lost one can only be fixed on the server.

## Things to know

- Every exam is **20 minutes** long.
- The nightly exams are open only from **11:00 PM to 11:20 PM** (Dhaka time). To try a sitting you need to be there at that time.
- A **written** exam has to be written on paper, scanned and uploaded **inside** those 20 minutes.
- The exam names, descriptions and questions are drafts for you to read and correct. Please mark anything that is wrong.
- The nightly exams cover the next seven days. After that, ask Shahriar to reset the test copy.
