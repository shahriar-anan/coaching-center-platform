# What is new on the site: November 2026

Two things changed in the staff area: **direct enrolment** now records money, and **adding exams to a package** is much faster. Package cards also show long prices in full instead of cutting them off.

Nothing changed for students who buy online. Existing students, orders and exams were left as they were.

---

## 1. Direct enrolment

Until now, direct enrolment gave a student access and recorded only a reason. It now also records what the student paid, what is still due, and when the next payment is due. This works the same way for **exams** and for **packages**.

**Only the Master Admin** can enrol a student this way and record payments.

### Where to find it

- **Direct enrolment** in the staff menu, to pick any student and any exam or package.
- The **Direct enrolment** tab on an exam's page or a package's page, to enrol someone in that exam or package.

### The three payment types

| Type | Use it when | What you enter |
|---|---|---|
| **Full payment** | The student pays everything now | Agreed total, method, transaction reference, payment date |
| **Installments** | The student pays in parts | The same, plus the number of installments, the amount of each, and the next installment date |
| **No charge** | No money is taken, for example a scholarship | A reason |

### How to enrol a student

1. Choose the student and the exam or package.
2. Choose the payment type.
3. Check the **Agreed total**. It starts at the standard price, including any active discount. You can raise or lower it for this student, and the form shows the difference.
4. Choose the method: Cash, bKash, Nagad, Rocket or Bank.
5. Enter the **Transaction reference**. For cash the site fills in a number for you, and you can replace it.
6. For installments, set the plan (details below).
7. Press **Review and enrol**, check the summary, then **Confirm and enrol**.

The student gets **full access straight away**, after the first payment.

### How installments work

- The number of installments **includes today's payment**. "3 installments" means today plus two more. You can set from 2 to 24.
- You set the amount per installment. The last one takes whatever is left, so the total always adds up.
- The next date is **one month after the payment date** unless you change it.

### The Installments page

**Installments** in the staff menu is the Master Admin's place to manage every plan. Every direct enrolment screen links to it. For each plan you can:

- See the agreed total, the amount paid, the amount due and the next date.
- **Record a payment** when the student pays the next part.
- **Change the unpaid schedule** if you agree new dates or amounts.
- **Void the latest payment** with a reason, if it was entered by mistake. A voided payment stays on record and its receipt number is never reused. Voiding is not a refund.
- **Restore access** on a plan if access was removed.

### Receipts

Every payment gets its **own receipt with its own number**. It shows the total, the amount paid so far, the amount due and the next date. The receipt reads, for example, "Installment 1 of 3".

Students find their receipts under **My Orders → Offline purchases**, and can download each one as a PDF. A notification is sent for each payment. The receipt is also emailed if the student's email address is verified.

### Notifications and reminders

| Event | Student | Master Admin |
|---|---|---|
| A payment is recorded | Notification and receipt | Shown on the Installments page |
| 3 days before a due date | Notification and email | Notification and email |
| Payment overdue | Notification and email the day after the due date, then weekly until paid | The same |
| Exam date and time | Email on enrolment, for each exam that has not ended | |

The student's notifications appear under the **Exams** tab. The Master Admin's appear under a new **Installments** tab in the notification centre.

### If a payment is late

Nothing happens to the student's access automatically. The Master Admin is reminded and decides whether to remove access by hand.

### Warnings you may see on the form

- **The student is on the package's roster.** A student added to a package without payment cannot also be enrolled with a payment. Remove them from the roster first, then enrol them. The reverse also applies: a student enrolled directly cannot be added to the roster.
- **The student already has this exam or package another way.** The form tells you which package or route gives them access and any money still owed. This is only a warning, and you can still enrol them. Any price difference is yours to settle through the agreed total.
- **The student is still paying for an exam and you enrol them in a package with that exam.** The exam's plan does not close. Its balance stays owed, and the warning says so. For now this has to be settled by hand.

### Limits to be aware of

- Students cannot pay an installment online. The Master Admin records each payment.
- Refunds for these payments are handled outside the site.
- Direct enrolment is one student at a time.
- These payments stay separate from online orders and from Staff pay.
- Enrolments made before this update are unchanged and have no payment records.

## 2. Adding exams to a package

Previously exams had to be added one at a time. You can now find and add many in one go.

### How to add exams

1. Open the package and go to its **Exams** tab.
2. Press **Add existing exam**. A wide window opens, with filters and results on the left and your selected exams on the right.
3. Narrow the list by **category** (with its subcategories), **package**, **type**, **mode**, **status**, **phase** or **name**.
4. Tick the exams you want, or use **Select all** for everything matching the filters.
5. Check the list on the right, then press **Add**, and confirm.

### Good to know

- You can add up to **500 exams at a time**.
- Exams already in the package, withdrawn exams and exams that no longer exist are skipped. The site lists what it skipped and why, and the rest are still added.
- An exam can be in several packages.
- Draft exams can be added, and students see them only after they are published.
- **On a published package, the change waits until the Master Admin publishes the package.** Students who hold the package then get the new exams.
- The package page now shows every exam in the package, however many there are, and you can reorder all of them.
